Drop D vs Double Drop D Tuning

Both lower the bass string, but Double Drop D also changes the high E string.

Quick answer: Drop D is D A D G B E; Double Drop D is D A D G B D. Choose Drop D for familiar upper-string shapes and Double Drop D for matching low and high D drones.

At-a-glance comparison

FeatureDrop DDouble Drop D
NotesD A D G B ED A D G B D
Changed from standardString 6Strings 6 and 1
Main advantageOne-finger bass power chordsLow and high D drones

What stays familiar

Drop D leaves the upper five strings in standard tuning, so most open chord shapes remain usable with only the sixth string reconsidered. Double Drop D changes the first string too, altering any shape that normally uses high E.

Sound and playing

Both tunings give a strong low D. Double Drop D adds symmetry and is useful for modal fingerpicking, suspended voicings, and melodies that resolve to the open first-string D.

Setup impact

Both changes lower tension and are safe for ordinary experimentation. The two changed strings in Double Drop D may require a second tuning pass as total tension settles.

How to choose

  1. Start with Drop D if you want heavy riffs or minimal chord changes.
  2. Choose Double Drop D when an open high D benefits the arrangement.
  3. Use the dedicated tuner preset and recheck after changing strings.
