Tune one string at a time

  1. Confirm that your instrument and string set are intended for standard 12-string tuning.
  2. Select a course member. Mute the other member and the remaining strings, then pluck the selected string.
  3. Check the octave alongside the pitch. Tune the other member separately, even when the two targets match.
  4. Repeat all six pairs after the first pass.

The microphone hears pitch. You choose which physical string you are checking. For the two matching pairs, pick a consistent first and second string. The physical order of octave and main strings can vary by guitar. Raise the thin octave G gradually and check its target before turning its peg.
