About the neural model and privacy

Uses the open-source TONE3000 WebAssembly engine, built on Neural Amp Modeler Core. The engine runs in an AudioWorklet. This separate lab is not yet connected to the live pedalboard, saved rigs or WAV renderer. No microphone, camera or account is required.

A capture models the equipment used to create it; it does not reconstruct a magnetic pickup from acoustic microphone audio. Use an amp-only capture with cabinet filtering enabled, or a full-rig capture with cabinet filtering disabled. No third-party captures are bundled.

The runtime must be prepared on the same host before this experiment can run. Setup and licensing notes.
