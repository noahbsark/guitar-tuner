About Simple Guitar Tuner

A simple, fast guitar tuner built for beginner guitarists.

Simple Guitar Tuner is a free browser-based guitar tuner. It works with your device microphone and helps you tune standard guitar tuning, Drop D, DADGAD, Open G, Open D, and other common tunings.

The goal is to keep tuning simple: open the page, tap Start, pluck a string, and follow the meter.
