# Virtual Guitar Studio product brief

Audience: guitarists who want to explore amplified sounds without an amplifier or a cable, particularly beginners. Secondary audience: players comparing gear or practicing a recording.

Primary journey: choose a recording or microphone mode, hear a preset, adjust the tone, save/share/export. A generated demo provides a permission-free first experience. Record-only microphone mode needs no headphones; continuous monitoring requires headphones. Speaker echo experiments are isolated from the audio feedback loop.

Keep the core tool free, local and account-free. Gear discovery is optional and separated from playing. Use truthful affiliate disclosure; physical gear recommendations are approximate tonal analogues. The Amazon tag already configured by the owner is active; other retailer links must remain ordinary until approved.

Constraints: static Cloudflare Pages site, no production changes until review, no paid backend, no audio uploads, no unlicensed models or recordings. Prioritize sound quality and first-use comprehension over new feature count.
