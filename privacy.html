Tuner Guide About Privacy

Privacy Policy

This tuner is designed to be privacy-friendly.

Microphone use

The tuner uses your microphone to detect pitch in your browser. Audio is processed locally on your device.

Your audio is not recorded, saved, uploaded, or sent to a server by this tuner.

Analytics and ads

This starter version does not include analytics or advertising code. If you add analytics, ads, or affiliate links later, update this page to explain what services you use.
