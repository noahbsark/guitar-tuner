Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Online Guitar Tuner handles information when you use this website.

Microphone access

The tuner requests microphone access so it can analyze the pitch of your guitar. Pitch analysis happens locally in your browser. Microphone audio is not recorded, saved, or uploaded by Online Guitar Tuner.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the website. Google Analytics may process information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, referring source, and interaction data. Google may use cookies or similar technologies where permitted by your consent choices.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services in Google’s partner-sites policy. You can also use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize, and measure ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services in Google’s partner-sites policy.

Your choices

You can restrict cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect advertising or some website features. Where required, the site may present a consent message that lets you manage advertising choices.
