Questions, corrections, or feature ideas?

Contact Online Guitar Tuner

Questions, factual corrections, broken-link reports, accessibility feedback, advertising inquiries, and feature ideas are welcome. If you are reporting a problem, include the page URL, your device and browser, and what you expected to happen.

Email: lockedtabspro@gmail.com

For microphone issues, first confirm that the page uses HTTPS and that both your browser and operating system allow microphone access. The site cannot view or change those permissions for you.

Please do not send sensitive personal or financial information by email.

Reporting a tuner issue

Open a tuner issue email template

Copy these fields into your email: page address, browser and device, tuning preset, intended string, microphone status, detected note and expected result.
