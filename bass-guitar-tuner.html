Choose your bass

Four-string Standard is E1 A1 D2 G2. Five-string Standard adds B0 below E1. Choose the five-string preset only if your instrument uses the low-B layout.

Tune one string at a time

  1. Choose your preset and tap Start listening. Allow microphone access.
  2. Mute the other strings and pluck a single string gently. Let the initial attack settle.
  3. Check the note and octave before adjusting. Select the string manually if automatic target selection keeps moving.
  4. After tuning each string, check the entire bass again.

To tune without the microphone, tap Tune by ear and choose a string to hear its reference tone.

If the low B or E is hard to hear

Compare a clip-on with a published range covering low B.

Reduce background noise and move the microphone nearer the sound source. If needed, use a clean amplifier at a moderate volume or a compatible audio interface selected as your system input. Small speakers can make the lowest reference tones hard to hear. If the readout shows B1 or E2 instead of B0 or E1, check for an octave overtone before turning a peg.
