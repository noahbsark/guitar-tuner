In Drop D, the low E string loses about 21% of its tension and the other five stay unchanged. If the result feels comfortable, keep your current strings. If a lowered string rattles or goes sharp with your usual attack, compare starting gauges for drop tunings or read how to choose a replacement set.

What the percentage measures

For the same string and vibrating length, tension changes with the square of the frequency ratio. A ratio of 0.89 in pitch is about 0.79 in tension. The tool does not estimate pounds of tension, predict the effect of a different gauge or construction, or determine whether raised pitches are suitable for your guitar.
