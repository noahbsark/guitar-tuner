How this 528 Hz preset is calibrated

This preset places C5 at exactly 528 Hz in twelve-tone equal temperament. That makes A4 equal to 443.993 Hz, commonly rounded to 444 Hz on a chromatic tuner. The guitar strings remain in standard E A D G B E intervals, but every pitch is about 15.7 cents above A4 = 440 Hz calibration.

How to tune safely

  1. Select the 528 Hz preset and allow microphone access.
  2. Pluck each string separately and use small tuning-key movements.
  3. Because these targets are slightly higher than standard, approach each pitch slowly.
  4. Repeat all six strings after the total tension settles.
This preset raises every string slightly above its normal 440 Hz target. Stop and replace any damaged or heavily corroded string before tuning upward.

Where C5 appears on guitar

With this calibration, C5 at 528 Hz occurs at the eighth fret of the high E string and the thirteenth fret of the B string. Those fretted notes are useful checks, but fret pressure and intonation can move them slightly sharp or flat. Tune the open strings first.

Reference-pitch clarification

“528 Hz tuning” can refer to different systems online. This page uses a specific, reproducible definition: C5 equals 528 Hz under equal temperament. It does not claim that the frequency provides medical or healing effects.
