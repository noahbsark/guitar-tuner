Check the note before turning a peg

  1. Find the note and octave you mean to tune. For example, the usual open A string is A2 at 110 Hz.
  2. Tap Start listening, allow microphone access, and pluck one string while muting the others.
  3. Wait for a steady note. Adjust a small amount only after the displayed note and octave match your intended target.

This tuner uses A4 = 440 Hz and analyzes audio in your browser. Aim for a clean signal between roughly 45 and 700 Hz. Small microphones may struggle with the lowest notes.
