Tune to the targets you entered

Choose a numbered string in the tuner and pluck it alone. Verify the detected octave before turning the peg. Select Tune by ear if you want to play a reference tone instead.

The tuner uses A4 = 440 Hz and supports targets from A1 through E5. Those are the tool’s limits; the usable range of each physical string depends on the guitar and strings. Copy the link and bookmark it to keep your setup. The link contains your six target pitches.

If your tuning already exists on the site, the retuning planner can compare it with the preset you are starting from. If you need to identify a pitch before choosing a target, use the chromatic tuner.
