Choose your seven-string tuning

Standard adds B1 below the familiar E2 A2 D3 G3 B3 E4 strings. Drop A lowers string 7 by two semitones to A1. Strings 6–1 stay the same.

Tune with your microphone or by ear

  1. Choose Standard or Drop A.
  2. Tap Start listening and allow microphone access. Mute the other strings and pluck string 7 gently.
  3. Check the detected octave and follow the flat or sharp reading until the pitch settles. Select the string manually if automatic selection switches targets.
  4. Tune strings 6–1 and repeat the full pass.

To tune by ear, leave the microphone off, choose a numbered string target, and tap Play reference tone. Match the pitch by ear, then repeat for the next string.

If the lowest string is hard to detect

Move closer to the microphone, mute the six other strings and pluck with a moderate attack. Small speakers may barely reproduce A1 or B1. Try headphones for the reference tone or use the microphone reading.
