How it works

This beta uses real-time digital filters, saturation, compression and simulated speaker voicing. Acoustic mode reduces body resonance before amplification; electric mode boosts the quiet unplugged signal. Neither can perfectly reconstruct a magnetic pickup from a microphone.

Place the microphone near the strings, play in a quiet room and adjust input gain until the meter responds without clipping. For a recording with vocals or background music, the effects apply to the entire audio track; this version does not isolate the guitar.

Your microphone and selected recordings are processed locally in your browser. No audio uploads, account or paid service is required. Browser and device latency vary. Video export re-encodes the visible footage at up to 720p and 30 fps and replaces the entire audio track. Some browsers cannot export video.
